# Multisim — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026
**Developer:** ThoughtOcean Technologies

Multisim is a single-player game. We designed it to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Multisim does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The game does not connect to the internet.
- There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other device data.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (your creature or plant, DNA, your tribe and your cities) and your settings (music and sound)
are saved **only on your device**, using the standard iOS app storage. They never leave the device. Deleting the
app deletes this data.

## Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and collects no data, and is suitable for all ages.
