# Multisim — Support

Multisim is an evolution game by ThoughtOcean Technologies. You start as a tiny creature or a plant in the ocean,
build your body part by part, walk onto land with your tribe, and finally unite every city in the world.

## How to play

**Stage 1 — the ocean**

- Steer with the joystick on the left. Eat food (or other creatures, if you are a carnivore) to earn DNA and fill
  the bar at the top. Each full bar is a new mini stage: you grow bigger.
- Tap **Creature** to open the creature creator. Spend DNA on **Roll for parts**, then drag parts onto your body
  and drag the bones to shape it. The **complexity meter** shows how many more parts fit.
- Playing as a **plant**: your vines grow towards the sunlight. Put leaves, branches and traps on them in the plant
  creator, and use a Grasping Tendril to catch a creature and take it onto land.
- When all seven mini stages are done, give your creature hands and legs and evolve onto land.

**Stage 2 — the tribe**

- Gather food, build your town with **Build**, and grow your tribe.
- Four villages live nearby. Win them over with gifts, singing and dancing, or **Attack** and conquer them.
  Uniting all four takes you to the city stage.

**Stage 3 — the city**

- Train infantry, archers, tanks and fighters with gold. Tap a city to attack it or send it a gift.
- Unite every city in the world, by conquest or alliance, to win the game.

## Settings

Tap the gear in the top-right corner to pause. There you can turn **Music** and **Sound** on or off, change their
volume with the sliders, or reset your progress.

## Frequently asked

**My progress disappeared.** Progress is saved only on the device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

**There is no sound.** Check the Music and Sound buttons and sliders in Settings, and the iPhone's silent switch.

**The camera is too close or too far.** Pinch with two fingers to zoom. In the city stage you can also use the
+ and − buttons.
